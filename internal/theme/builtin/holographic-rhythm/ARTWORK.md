# Artwork provenance

All distributed raster assets were generated with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool for this package. The approved preview established the virtual singer, blue-to-pink hair, sky city and holographic projection direction. The Neko Coder theme in the read-only Codex Theme reference repository, used with ReTheme, informed the environment; its files are not included in this package.

PNGs are downscaled for their display roles and compressed while preserving transparency. The hero and wallpapers use optimized lossless PNG encoding to retain hair and sky gradients. Small icons and ornaments use optimized palettes. Light and dark wallpapers retain matching composition and framing. UI controls, labels and interactions remain Sailry-owned.
