# Role avatar source

`gaze.json` (role avatars) and `shape-grid.json` (team avatars) are the Gaze
and Shape Grid style definitions from DiceBear Styles v10.6.0
(https://github.com/dicebear/styles, `src/`), unchanged. SHA-256:

- `gaze.json`: `03d83e72adc01c52af10337803d6d66a034d43648e5870c8764d1a7fdbdd30dd`
- `shape-grid.json`: `0108088c7e384ad4a8cceca1581b76235420ef0cfc56a111d69b0b43b080edeb`

Gaze and Shape Grid by DiceBear (https://www.dicebear.com) are licensed under
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each definition
repeats this in its `meta` block.

The pictures are rendered from it at run time by
`github.com/dicebear/dicebear-go/v10` (MIT), listed in
`third_party_licenses/go-modules.md`.
