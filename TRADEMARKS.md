covers the code, not the Sailry name, logos or other brand assets. Section 6 of
the license states that it grants no permission to use the licensor's trade
names, trademarks or service marks.

## Allowed without permission

- Refer to Sailry by name to describe your project accurately, for example
  "a fork of Sailry", "built on Sailry" or "a plugin for Sailry".
- Distribute unmodified official builds with their original branding.
- Use the name and logo in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews about Sailry.

## Requires written permission

- Distributing a modified version, fork or derivative product under the Sailry
  name or with the Sailry logo.
- Using Sailry in the name of a product, service, company, domain or app store
  listing.
- Implying that a project, service or organization is official, endorsed by or
  affiliated with Sailry.

A fork or modified build must use its own name and logo and remove the Sailry
logos from its application, installers and listings. You may still state that
it is based on Sailry.

Third-party names and logos shown in Sailry, such as model provider brands,
belong to their respective owners.

To request permission, [contact the maintainers](https://github.com/sailry) or
[open an issue](https://github.com/sailry/sailry-harness/issues).
