# Code of conduct

Sailry welcomes people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Treat others with respect, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, and give
constructive feedback. Respect personal boundaries and requests to end contact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, publication of private
information, spam and retaliation against someone who raises a concern are not
acceptable.

This applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project-managed community spaces. Maintainers may warn participants, remove
content or restrict participation according to the conduct and its impact.

## Reporting a concern

[Ask a maintainer for a private contact method](https://github.com/sailry/sailry-harness/issues/new?title=Private%20contact%20request).
Do not include names, personal information or incident details in that public
request. Share the details privately once a maintainer provides a channel.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
