# Reicon icons

Source: https://github.com/dqev/reicon/tree/109d994d810342a998e79ab65572c68176bc2e0a

All 2,676 Outline paths from `data/icon-data.json` are embedded in
`assets/icons/reicon/catalog.json`, keyed by `category/name`. Paths are unchanged;
the asset loader adds a 24 × 24 SVG wrapper. The portable name index is
`crates/protocol/src/plugin/desktop/icon_names.txt`. Source data SHA-256:
`a50139172599b15f1e198f66efe2716e39924e763fd2282eb5155857efe3f3ce`.

The selected individual SVG files remain available under their existing aliases;
their filenames map to Sailry's project identities and interface icons. Reicon is licensed under MIT
(`reicon-MIT.txt`). Upstream credits Solar Icons by 480 Design (CC BY 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and Zappicon
(https://zappicon.com/). Those upstream attributions are retained here.
