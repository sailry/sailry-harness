Reicon
https://github.com/dqev/reicon/tree/109d994d810342a998e79ab65572c68176bc2e0a

Selected Outline paths from data/icon-data.json are unchanged and wrapped in 24 by 24 SVG elements. Application filenames map to the upstream icons listed in sources.json.

Reicon base icons include elements from Solar Icons by 480 Design (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, https://solar-icons.vercel.app/) and Zappicon (https://zappicon.com/).
